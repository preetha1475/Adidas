Why This Project?
Adidas is more than a sportswear brand — it is a story of innovation, challenges, and comeback. This project explores Adidas’ US sales data to uncover meaningful insights into product performance, customer purchasing patterns, retailer contributions, and profitability. By transforming raw sales data into interactive visualizations and actionable insights, this project demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses make smarter decisions and drive growth.

Operating Profit by Product

Monthly Profit Trend

Month-over-Month Sales Growth (%)

This chart calculates month-over-month sales growth as (current month sales − previous month sales) ÷ previous month sales × 100. Positive bars mean growth; negative bars mean decline.

Average Unit Price by Product

This chart calculates the average recorded Price per Unit for each product in the filtered transactions. It does not measure discounts or prove that price caused a change in demand.

Historical Sales and Six-Month Baseline Forecast

How to interpret these analyses
Growth compares each month with the previous month. Anomaly flags use an interquartile-range rule and indicate unusual values, not necessarily errors. The forecast is a simple linear trend and should be treated as a baseline, not a promise. This dataset has no discount column, so discount impact cannot be measured directly.

03 / FROM DATA TO DECISIONS

What can the data teach us?

Business analytics helps transform raw transactions into decisions about products, retail partnerships, geography, sales channels, and inventory planning.

Product strategy
Compare revenue, units sold, and profit to understand which products contribute most to retail performance.
Regional strategy
Identify regions with higher recorded sales and investigate possible differences in demand and distribution.
Channel strategy
Compare online, outlet, and in-store sales using the data. Revenue alone does not explain customer behaviour or costs.
Important limitation
This dataset represents US retail transactions from a limited period. It cannot independently prove why Adidas rose or fell globally. Historical explanations require separate evidence.
